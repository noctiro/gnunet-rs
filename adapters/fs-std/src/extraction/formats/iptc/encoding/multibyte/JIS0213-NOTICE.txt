The packed derivative contains all 94 rows of plane 1 followed by the
assigned rows of plane 2, in row and cell order. Each entry uses three
little-endian bytes. Zero indicates an unassigned cell. Values from
0x110000 index the two-scalar mappings in plane 1. For ISO-IR 229, the
original JIS X 0213:2000 association maps 2-93-27 to U+9B1D (WG2 N2926 T.10).
