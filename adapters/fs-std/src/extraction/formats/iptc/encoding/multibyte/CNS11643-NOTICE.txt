CNS 11643-1992 mapping data

Ministry of Digital Affairs, Taiwan, 2026. CNS 11643 Chinese Standard
Interchange Code Full Character Set (CNS11643中文標準交換碼全字庫), Unicode
mapping tables dated 2026-10-27. The source is made available to the public
under the Open Government Data License, version 1.0.

Source: https://www.cns11643.gov.tw/
Dataset: https://data.gov.tw/dataset/5961
License: https://data.gov.tw/en/license
Archive: https://data.cns11643.gov.tw/opendata/MapingTables.zip
Archive SHA-256: 605cadc2074cb5399bf03a268ab1f66a52384f08acb27021178c13d041cb87d2

cns11643.bin packs all registered graphic positions as three-byte
little-endian Unicode scalars in ascending plane, row and cell order.
Plane 1 packs assigned ranges only. Planes 2 through 7 each begin at
21/21 and end at the last position registered in ISO-IR 172 and 183–187.
Later allocations beyond those boundaries are omitted.

The Ministry's duplicate-closure report supplies the retained Unicode
values for original codes 4-2361, 4-276A, 4-287D, 4-2A6E, 4-3042, 4-385C,
4-4458, 4-6339, 5-234B, 5-756C, 6-2A21, 6-5C2F and 7-4159.
https://www.cns11643.gov.tw/files/files/CNS11643_CLOSE1111103.pdf

The mapping retains three Ministry private-use values at 3-6168, 4-225B
and 4-655F. Their appearance depends on the font.

Unihan_IRGSources.txt from the Unicode Character Database 17.0.0 supplies
the kIRG_TSource mappings for 5-5351, 6-2963 and 6-345D.
https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/ucd/Unihan.zip
