JIS C 6226-1978 (ISO-IR 42) mapping data

ISO registration: https://itscj.ipsj.or.jp/ir/042.pdf

The packed repertoire joins the position-to-CID mappings in Adobe's 78-H
(version 10.006) with the CID-to-Unicode mappings in Adobe-Japan1-UCS2
(version 10.002). The two sources and their SHA-256 digests are:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-type-tools/cmap-resources/master/Adobe-Japan1-7/CMap/78-H
bf1b3dcae13713e5c1ba7422c7803561d46762448ef433d06405b766f5142e73
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-type-tools/mapping-resources-pdf/master/pdf2unicode/Adobe-Japan1-UCS2
6a9693361647a37996312cc57071bb79f8c06411207be7c730a83fda1254cd82

The table follows row and cell order, with rows 0x21–0x27 followed by rows
0x30–0x73. Each three-byte little-endian word holds a Unicode scalar in its
low 21 bits; zero means unassigned. The next two bits select no variation
selector, U+FE00, U+E0100 or U+E0101, respectively.
